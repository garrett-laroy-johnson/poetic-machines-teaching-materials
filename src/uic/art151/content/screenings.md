Weekly textbook chapters are listed on each week's page. Alongside them, each module pairs technique with context: who makes this kind of work, and what is at stake in it.

**Module 2: Animation, Interaction, and Movement (Weeks 7–11)**

- Reading: *Aesthetic Programming*, Chapter 2, “Variable Geometry” (on emoji and the politics of representation) and Chapter 3, “Infinite Loops” (on time and the throbber)
- Group presentations: each group researches artists working in generative art, sound and music, or games and simulation (see the Group Presentation page)

**Module 3: Objects, Arrays, and Virtual Space (Weeks 12–15)**

- Reading: *Aesthetic Programming*, Chapter 6, “Object Abstraction”
- Reading: Legacy Russell, *Glitch Feminism: A Manifesto* (Verso, 2020), selections, on the online body and digital space
