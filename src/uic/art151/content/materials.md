**Textbook (free PDF on the course site):** Lauren McCarthy, Casey Reas, and Ben Fry, _Make: Getting Started with p5.js_.

**Readings:** selected chapters from Winnie Soon and Geoff Cox, _Aesthetic Programming: A Handbook of Software Studies_ (Open Humanities Press, 2020; open access), which pairs p5.js techniques with critical and cultural readings.

**External Course Website:** I host a website for all of my courses, which contains examples, links to video tutorials, lists of relevant functions and concepts by week, and artist screenings and explanations of the techniques we are learning.

**Software (free, browser-based):** the [p5.js Web Editor](https://editor.p5js.org/) and [New Art City](https://newart.city/). No software purchase is required for this course.

**Equipment:** a laptop or desktop computer (phones and tablets will not work), a reliable internet connection, and Zoom. Camera use is required during critique and tutorials.

Every tutorial is recorded and posted to Canvas.
