#### Course Format

This is a hybrid course. On **Tuesdays** we meet online over Zoom for tutorials, which are recorded and posted to Canvas. On **Thursdays** we meet in person for studio time, listening, and critique, so bring your laptop and headphones.

#### Studio Exercises (10%)

Most Thursdays you will build a short patch that puts that week's tutorial into practice (described on each week's page). Studio exercises are graded on effort: a complete, good-faith attempt earns full credit, even if it doesn't fully work.

#### Participation (10%)

As the UIC Department of Art describes, a studio course is built on active participation: making together, thinking together, and learning from one another. Participation means coming to class prepared, sharing your work in progress, asking questions, and engaging thoughtfully in listening sessions and in critique of your classmates' work. You can participate by speaking in class or in the Zoom chat.
