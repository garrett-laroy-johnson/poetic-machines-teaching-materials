For this class you will need:

- a modern Windows or Mac laptop (Chromebooks, tablets, and phones do not suffice)
- a pair of headphones for classroom work sessions
- a Max/MSP subscription license for the duration of the course (provided through your course fee)
- other materials you may wish to use in projects (a USB game controller, a portable webcam, etc.)

**Texts and tutorials:** Max's built-in tutorials and reference pages, plus the patches and recorded tutorials posted to this site and Canvas. Recommended reading: Pauline Oliveros, *Deep Listening: A Composer's Sound Practice* (2005).

**Free and open-source alternative:** everything we build in Max transfers to [Pure Data](https://puredata.info/), a free, open-source environment created by Max's original author, Miller Puckette, so you can keep making work after the course and your license end.

**Listening:** each week includes listenings (see the schedule). Genre anchors for the course include:

- drone: Éliane Radigue, Pauline Oliveros, La Monte Young
- ambient and early synthesis: Laurie Spiegel, Suzanne Ciani, Brian Eno
- IDM: Autechre, Aphex Twin
- techno and house: Drexciya, Underground Resistance, Jeff Mills, Phuture and Chicago house

**Hearing safety and access:** protect your hearing. Keep headphone and speaker levels moderate, start every patch with the volume down, and use a `live.gain~` or other level control in everything you build. If you are d/Deaf or hard of hearing, or experience sound differently, we will work out visual, tactile, and other ways to engage with the material; let me know what works for you.
