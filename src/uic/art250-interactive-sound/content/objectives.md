By the end of this course, you will be able to:

- Build synthesis instruments in Max/MSP using oscillators, additive and subtractive synthesis, filters, amplitude modulation, LFOs, and envelopes. *(Module 1; Midterm)*
- Structure sound in time with timers, counters, sequencers, and presets. *(Module 1; Midterm)*
- Record, process, and play back samples. *(Module 2; Final Project)*
- Map gestural interfaces (keyboard, mouse, game controllers, camera) to sound for live performance and installation. *(Module 2; Final Project)*
- Describe sound, and your strategies for working with it, in a critical listening vocabulary drawn from drone, ambient, IDM, and techno. *(listenings; critiques)*
- Give specific, usable critique and act on the critique you receive. *(critiques)*
