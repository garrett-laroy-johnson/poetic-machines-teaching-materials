Readings are short selections, posted on Canvas, and discussed during Thursday studio sessions.

- **Module 1:** Tara Rodgers, *Pink Noises: Women on Electronic Music and Sound* (Duke University Press, 2010), selected interviews; Pauline Oliveros, *Deep Listening: A Composer's Sound Practice* (2005), selections
- **Module 2, sampling (Week 9):** Kodwo Eshun, *More Brilliant than the Sun: Adventures in Sonic Fiction* (1998), selections
- **Weeks 12–15, interactive sound and generative systems:** George E. Lewis, “Too Many Notes: Computers, Complexity, and Culture in *Voyager*,” *Leonardo Music Journal* 10 (2000), on an interactive computer system that improvises with human musicians
