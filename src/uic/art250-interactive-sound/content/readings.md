Readings are short selections, posted on Canvas, and discussed during Thursday studio sessions.

- **Module 1:** Tara Rodgers, *Pink Noises: Women on Electronic Music and Sound* (Duke University Press, 2010), selected interviews; Pauline Oliveros, *Deep Listening: A Composer's Sound Practice* (2005), selections
- **Module 2, sampling:** Mark Katz, *Capturing Sound: How Technology Has Changed Music* (University of California Press, 2004; rev. 2010), “Music in 1s and 0s: The Art and Politics of Digital Sampling”
- **Module 2, interactive sound:** George E. Lewis, “Too Many Notes: Computers, Complexity, and Culture in *Voyager*,” *Leonardo Music Journal* 10 (2000), on an interactive computer system that improvises with human musicians
- **Weeks 12–15, IDM and techno:** Kodwo Eshun, *More Brilliant than the Sun: Adventures in Sonic Fiction* (1998), selections
