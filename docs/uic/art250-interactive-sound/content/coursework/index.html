UIC | ART 250 | Introduction to Interactive Sound

Key Concepts:

Course Format

This is a hybrid course. On Tuesdays we meet online over Zoom for tutorials, which are recorded and posted to Canvas. On Thursdays we meet in person for studio time, listening, readings discussion, and critique, so bring your laptop and headphones. The last Tuesday of the semester is also online; final presentations take place in person on the last Thursday.

Studio Exercises (10%)

Most Thursdays you will build a short patch that puts that week's tutorial into practice (described on each week's page). Studio exercises are graded on effort: a complete, good-faith attempt earns full credit, even if it doesn't fully work.

Participation (10%)

As the UIC Department of Art describes, a studio course is built on active participation: making together, thinking together, and learning from one another. Participation means coming to class prepared, sharing your work in progress, asking questions, and engaging thoughtfully in listening sessions and in critique of your classmates' work. You can participate by speaking in class or in the Zoom chat.

Late Work

Studio exercises may be submitted up to one week after the end of their module for half credit; after that, they receive no credit. Projects (Print Poems, Midterm, and Final Project) may be submitted late with a penalty of one letter grade for each week they are late.
